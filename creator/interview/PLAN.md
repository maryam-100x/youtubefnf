# The setup interview

*Asked by the main chat during the setup (`SETUP.md`), a few questions at a time, as a conversation: follow up on what is
interesting, skip what is already answered. The creator's answers go into `round-01.md` **verbatim** (their words, under each
question); the chat's reading of them goes in a separate "Chat notes" section of that file, never mixed into the quotes, and then
into `creator/PROFILE.md`. Later interviews (after each script, for the creator's own view on the topic) go into the video's folder.*

## Round 1: the channel and the taste
1. What is your channel about, and what videos do you want us to make: long ones, Shorts, both? Any first topic in mind?
2. Do you already have a channel? What has worked on it so far, and what hasn't?
3. Which creators in your niche (or outside it) do you love watching, and what is it about their videos? (3-5 names; their videos
   become the corpus the scripts are measured against.)
4. What makes you click away from a video? What makes you stay?
5. How do you talk when you tell a friend about this topic? Any words you always use, any you would never say?
6. Should the narration say "I" (you telling the story), or tell it without a narrator?
7. Pace and mood: calm and cinematic, or fast and punchy? Any music you like under videos like these?
8. A colour for your channel's look (the accent on graphics)? Do you want a sign-off at the end of long videos (for example asking for
   a like or a comment), and in what words?
9. What else runs on this computer while we work (other heavy apps, editing, streaming)? When should a heavy render wait for your go?
10. How do you like to be talked to: very short, or with the why?

# standard/research: the craft research, sourced

The studies the standard files rest on: how top creators edit, each finding with its source and its reliability grade (every file
states its own grading scheme at the top). Research is evidence, not rules: the rules live in `rules/` and `standard/`, and these
files point to them.

- `LONGFORM_CRAFT.md`: how top commentary and documentary editors edit 8-20-minute narrated videos for retention (pace, holds, sound,
  music, structure), with measured cut rates and the reference slots to fill from the creator's own niche.
- `MOTION_CRAFT.md`: what top documentary and commentary channels do in motion graphics, how it is made, and the recipes to build it
  top-tier in code (Remotion + ffmpeg): easings, transitions, maps, type, finishing, tools and their prices.
- `MOTION_LAB.md`: the motion techniques built, rendered and judged on their frames, with the settings that worked and the bugs found.
- `AI_SLOP_VS_PREMIUM.md`: what makes a narrated video's visuals read as AI slop versus premium, what top channels do instead, and the
  redesign brief that follows (licences, platform policy, a checklist of tells).
- `FOOTAGE_SOURCING.md`: the method of finding, testing, grading and logging source material for any niche: news archives, stock
  libraries, public archives, the primary source's own material, YouTube search, licences and prices.
- `SHORTS_CRAFT.md`: how the best Shorts are edited for viewed-vs-swiped, average percentage viewed and loops, and how Shorts editing
  differs from long form.
- `SHORTS_EDITING.md`: every element of a Short with a job: the 9:16 safe-zone numbers, the platform changes, and the lab science
  behind acting a line out, turned into build rules.
- `STUDY_SHORTS_REFS.md`: nineteen top reference Shorts measured (cuts, shot lengths, camera motion, words a minute, voice over the
  rest, sound on cuts) and what their frames show.
- `SOUND_CRAFT.md`: sound design and mixing for narrated videos and Shorts: the effect kinds and what each marks, measured timing,
  levels and density, music beds, cut-outs and ducking, the narration chain, loudness targets, real clips in their own sound, 54 effect
  categories mapped to the house set, libraries and plugins with prices.
- `SFX_TASTE.md`: the Sound Test method (blind, levelled, by job) and what three rounds and the reviews of the videos that used them
  taught, job by job, about the house effects in `assets/house_sfx.zip`.
- `VOICE_CRAFT.md`: natural, consistent narration from an ElevenLabs clone: the causes of accent drift, whispery takes, clipped edges,
  tone shifts and AI-sounding lines, the vendor's evidence, writing for TTS, post-processing, the automatic take QA, and the
  alternatives benchmarked.
- `VOICE_OPENINGS.md`: the tired, whispered opening and the swallowed word, measured, and the evidence behind the unspoken `lead_in`
  and `tools/voice/voice_check.py`.
- `PACKAGING_CRAFT.md`: what wins YouTube titles (the studies, YouTube's own pages, a measured niche and our own CTR and traffic
  numbers), what descriptions, hashtags, chapters and tags do, Shorts packaging, and the step-by-step packaging and Test & Compare
  process for every video.
- `AI_TELLS.md`: the evidence catalogue of what makes text read as LLM-written (Wikipedia's field guide, peer-reviewed and large-data
  studies) plus practitioners' advice on writing narration for the ear.
- `VIEWER_AI_TELLS.md`: the script habits ordinary viewers name when they call a narration "a ChatGPT script", from one public thread,
  each with what a script does instead.
- `HUMAN_SCRIPT_STUDY.md`: the blind-test study of why a chat's narration reads as AI: the corpus, the measures, every judged round and
  the judge audit, the literature back-check, the content gate proof and the style-only pair test, and why a real person's view, not
  the wording, is what reads human.
- `LINGO.md`: the method for learning a niche's spoken lingo from its creators' captions and the creator's own words: the corpus, the
  numbers to read, what real creators do, the complaint kinds `script_lint` catches, a 30-line rewrite exercise and its lessons.
- `LINGO_MEASURED.md`: the word-level measure (log-odds with a Dirichlet prior): what AI-written scripts say far more than human
  creators (names, numbers, "says", full titles, dates) and what they miss ("this", "we", the future, qualifiers, the niche's plain
  words), with neutral rates.

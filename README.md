# Studio: a Claude-run video studio for YouTube

This repo turns a Claude chat into a full video partner: it researches, writes the script, voices it with your AI voice, edits the
picture in code (Remotion), places the sound and music, checks every frame and every word before a render, and hands you the finished
video. Long-form documentary-style videos and Shorts, for any niche.

## Start (3 steps)
1. Install the **Claude desktop app** and sign in (a paid Claude plan; Max is recommended because a video is a lot of work).
   On Windows, also install **Git for Windows** first (https://git-scm.com/download/win), so the app's Code tab can run commands.
2. Make an **empty folder** for your studio (for example `C:\Studio\repo` on Windows or `~/Studio/repo` on a Mac), open the
   desktop app's **Code** tab, and choose that empty folder.
3. Paste this repo's link into the chat and say: **"Clone this into this folder and let's get started. You have my OK to download and install everything the setup and our videos need."**
   (That last sentence is your one go for every download and install: the chat's safety rules take it only from your own words in
   the chat, and with it the chat never asks you again for each program or file.)

The chat checks your computer, installs what is missing and sets everything up with you one step at a time. Then you make videos
together. You need an **ElevenLabs** account for the voice (a paid plan; the chat walks you through it). Music is made by the chat
in code, or you pick your own.

## New versions
The studio gets new versions now and then (`CHANGELOG.md` says what each brings), and your main chat tells you when one is out. To
bring one in, tell your main chat: **"Update the studio. You have my OK to download and install what it needs."** It brings the new
version in and keeps everything that's yours: your rules and taste, your voice, your videos, your settings. It sets up anything new.

## For the Claude chat that just cloned this
Read `CLAUDE.md` now, then follow `SETUP.md` from its first step. One step at a time: say what to do, check it worked, then the next.

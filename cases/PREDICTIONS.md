## How to write a prediction
- **Before the piece goes to the creator**, never after any word of theirs on it. Once written, a row is never edited; a changed mind
  is a new row marked as one.
- **One row per item that could draw a note**: a section, a graphic, a run of sound events, a take, a script passage, a picture under a
  line. Each row names its craft. Then the expected overall verdict ("passes with at most one note", "two or three picture notes, no
  rework", "a rebuild").
- **Each row says PASS, or FLAG with what and why**, the book entries it leans on, and a light risk where one is felt. For a choice
  between options, a percentage per option, with a branch for "none of these".
- **Honest, not hopeful.** When the chat's own risk note says a thing will be flagged, the row says FLAG, not PASS anyway. A pick the
  creator doubted when picking it is that piece's main risk. A light risk is a prediction, not a fix list: it is not fixed before
  sending unless the books say it should be.
- **Measure what an eye can't see.** Faults below what a look at the frames shows (a 1-px step at an edge, a sound a few frames off its
  event, a level) are predicted from a measurement, not a judgement.
- **For an idea, ask where it came from.** A stock device pasted on reads generic whatever the finish; before predicting a pass, ask
  whether it was thought of for this video or taken from a kit.

## How to score
- When the verdict comes: every row HIT or MISS, with the creator's words verbatim; the overall HIT or MISS.
- A row predicted PASS on a piece the creator passed whole is a HIT. A note on something no row predicted is a MISS of the overall and
  a new case.
- **Every MISS becomes a case in its book the same turn**, its ID written into the row. A MISS on a line already in a book is marked
  as one (the gravest kind: `README.md`).
- When two or more misses share a pattern, one line **What the misses teach** under the block says it, and the lesson goes into the
  book's line too.
- The score at the top is updated in the same turn.

## The format of a block
```
### <YYYY-MM-DD> · <video> · <what goes to the creator>
**Expected overall:** <the verdict expected>. **The line from the books:** <the entries leaned on, e.g. PI41, CO12>.
| # | Craft | What | Predicted |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | picture | <the item, in plain words> | PASS / FLAG <what, why>; light risk: <...>; <% per option for a choice> |

**Scored <YYYY-MM-DD>:** the creator: "<their words>". Overall HIT / MISS. Rows: 1 HIT, 2 MISS -> PI58, ...
**What the misses teach:** <the pattern, when there is one>
```
